## 🧠 About This Repository

This repository documents my work for **MSML 633: Agentic AI** at the **University of Maryland, College Park**.

The course explores the principles and implementation of **agentic AI systems** — AI systems capable of reasoning, planning, interacting with tools and environments, maintaining context, and executing multi-step tasks toward a goal.

A major component of the course consists of **five cumulative projects**, where concepts introduced throughout the semester are progressively combined to build increasingly capable agentic systems.

The repository serves both as a record of my coursework and as a practical exploration of how modern AI agents are designed, implemented, evaluated, and improved.
